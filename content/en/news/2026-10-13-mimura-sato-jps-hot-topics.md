Collaborative research by Tomohiro Mimura, Prof. Katsuhiko Sato of the University of Toyama, and colleagues has been featured in JPS Hot Topics, a research highlights website published by the Physical Society of Japan.

The study reveals how rods with periodic surface corrugations can form helicoidal stacks only at specific angles. The highlight article, "Only Certain Angles Allow Layer-by-Layer Stacking of Corrugated Rods", explains these findings.
